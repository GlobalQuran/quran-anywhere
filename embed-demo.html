Reading Surah Al-Baqarah slowly

By Amina · 4 November · 6 min read

I started the year with a simple habit: a few verses each morning, slowly, with a cup of tea. The full surah is below — you can read, scroll and listen right here without leaving the page.

Embedded with two lines of HTML. Drag the gold ribbon to move through the surah, or press play to listen. (If Global Quran ever requires API keys, add data-api-key="YOUR_KEY" to the div.)

What strikes me every time is how the first verses set out who the Book is for. I like to read the translation aloud and then sit with a single word for a minute. The gold ribbon on the right lists every ayah number; drag it to scrub quickly through a long surah.

If you would like to follow along, tap a verse to save it, copy it, or play from there.

A few notes

  • Read the verses before reading commentary.
  • Keep one word as your focus for the day.
  • Come back to the same page the next morning.
