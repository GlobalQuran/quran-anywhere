Privacy

What the website and our apps do with your information, in plain words. Each app has its own section.

Website Meta Quest Apple Vision Pro

The short version

Global Quran needs no account, has no advertising and sells nothing. You can sign in on the website if you want your saved verses on every device; that is the only time we keep anything about you. It does not follow you around other sites.

  • No sign-in needed. You never have to give us a name or an email to read or listen. Signing in on the website is optional (see Website). The apps for Meta Quest and Apple Vision Pro have no accounts at all.
  • No ads, no selling, no sharing for marketing.
  • Your places stay with you. Your settings, your saved verses and where you stopped are kept on your own device. They only reach our servers if you choose to sign in.
  • Only the website counts visits, anonymously, and in Europe and the UK only if you say yes. The apps for Meta Quest and Apple Vision Pro do not.

What our servers see (every app)

To show a verse and play its recitation, the website and the apps download the Quran text, translations, fonts and audio from Global Quran servers (api.globalquran.com, cdn.globalquran.com, audio.globalquran.com). Like any website, those servers receive your IP address and the file asked for (for example, which surah), and keep ordinary, short-lived server logs used only to run and protect the service. We do not link these requests to who you are, and we do not sell or share them. Everything is served through Cloudflare, which handles the requests on our behalf under its own privacy terms.

Website globalquran.com

We use Google Analytics to count visits and to see which features are used (for example which surahs are opened, whether audio is played, and which settings are changed), so we can improve the reader.

It stores a small cookie on this site, is never used for advertising, and never records what you type, your list of saved verses, or anything that identifies you. It does note which verses are saved, copied or played (not by whom), so we can see which verses are the most loved. You can change your mind at any time, here or in Settings.

Your choice

  • In the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland nothing is loaded or stored until you press Allow. Elsewhere it runs from the start and you can switch it off in Settings (Share anonymous usage statistics).
  • If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, statistics stay off.
  • Turning it off stops everything being sent and deletes the Google Analytics cookies.
  • We do not turn on Google signals or advertising features, and we do not use user IDs.

On your device

Your choices (translation, font, theme, reciter), your saved verses and the place you stopped are stored in your browser on your device. They are not sent to us unless you sign in.

If you sign in (optional)

Signing in lets you keep your saved verses, the place you stopped and a few settings (translation, font, theme, reciter and similar) on every device. The reader only offers it when you save a verse, in the menu, or when you come back; closing the offer hides it for a few days, and after three times it stops asking.

  • What we keep: your email address and name (from Google or Apple if you use them, or what you type if you use an email code), your saved verses, the place you stopped, and those settings. Nothing else about your reading, and no listening history.
  • Why: only to give you those things on your other devices and to let you into your account. Never for advertising and never sold or shared.
  • Google and Apple are used only to sign you in; they tell us your name and email address. If you use an email code, we email you a six-digit code (it expires after ten minutes).
  • Cookies: signing in sets a cookie so you stay signed in for up to 30 days. Signing out removes it, and your saved verses stay on that device.
  • Embedded readers on other websites sign in through a small pop-up on login.globalquran.com. The website that embeds the reader never sees your password, code or email address.
  • Deleting it: ask us on the Contact page and we will delete your account and everything stored with it.

Meta Quest Global Quran VR

Nothing about you is collected.

  • No accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not use your camera or microphone and does not save your settings or reading history.
  • Hand tracking, controllers and, if you choose the mixed-reality view, passthrough are handled on your headset in real time. They are not recorded, stored or sent to anyone.
  • It asks our servers only for the verses and audio it shows (see What our servers see above).

The same policy is also kept on its own page, quest.globalquran.com/privacy.html, which is the address given to Meta.

Apple Vision Pro Global Quran Vision

Nothing about you is collected. Its App Store privacy label says Data Not Collected.

  • No account, no advertising, no analytics, no cookies and no tracking.
  • Hand tracking is optional, asks your permission, and is used only on the device to turn the verse with a flick of the hand. It is never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Offline. When you choose to save a surah or the whole Quran, the text, fonts, translation and recitations are kept on your device (and left out of backups). You are asked first, you can see what is saved, and you can remove it at any time in Settings or by deleting the app.
  • Sharing a verse opens the system share sheet with a globalquran.com link. To have the preview picture ready, the app also asks our servers to prepare it for that verse, with the translation and reciter you chose. Nothing about you is in that request.
  • Optional contributions are handled entirely by Apple's In-App Purchase. We never see your payment details, and giving unlocks nothing: every feature is free.
  • Verses and audio come from our servers (see What our servers see above).

Global Quran Vision is published on the App Store by Noora Labs Ltd.
