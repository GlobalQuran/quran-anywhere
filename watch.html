Link your watch or TV

Sign in on the Global Quran app for Apple Watch, Wear OS, Apple TV or Android TV by typing the code it shows. Signing in is optional: the apps play everything without it.

Enter the code from your watch or TV

  1. On a watch, open Global Quran, then More, Account, Sign in. On a TV, open Global Quran, then Settings, Account, Sign in. It shows a code such as KQRM-48.
  2. Sign in here with the same account you use on the website and the phone apps.
  3. Type the code below. The watch or TV signs itself in within a few seconds.

Google, Apple or an email code. A window opens on the Global Quran sign-in page.

Signed in as Not you? Sign out

What this does

A watch or TV gets the same sign-in as the phone apps. A watch keeps only the place where you stopped (surah and ayah) in step with your other devices. A TV keeps that place and also your listening settings (reciter, translation, Arabic text, light or dark and the background), so it looks and sounds the same as your other devices. You can sign out, or delete your account, in the app under Account. See the privacy page.
